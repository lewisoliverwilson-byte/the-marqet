# Claude for Recruiters — 8-Week Newsletter Plan

**Platform:** Start on LinkedIn Newsletter (built-in distribution). Move to Beehiiv if growth stalls below 50 new subscribers/week by Week 5.
**Cadence:** One issue per week, same day and time each week. Thursday 8am local time is a strong default.
**Length:** 400–600 words per issue. Short enough to read in 5 minutes, long enough to be genuinely useful.
**CTA:** Every issue ends with one CTA — either the HR & Recruiting Pack or the newsletter subscription link. Never both.

## General newsletter rules

1. **Personal voice.** Write in first person. "I use this for…" not "Recruiters can use this for…"
2. **Concrete over vague.** Show an example, don't describe one.
3. **One idea per issue.** Don't combine two tips into one email. Save it for next week.
4. **Short subject lines.** Under 50 characters. No emojis unless your audience uses them.
5. **Reply-worthy.** End each issue with a question ("What's the most time-consuming part of your workflow right now?"). Replies are the signal that people are reading.
6. **Checkpoint at Week 5:** If you have fewer than 150 subscribers, pause and reassess the profession before continuing.
