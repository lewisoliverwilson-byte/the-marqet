# TikTok & Instagram Strategy — Claude for Recruiters

These platforms are pure top-of-funnel. The job is not to sell — it's to create the "wait, Claude can do THAT?" moment that sends people to Google and eventually to /recruiter or /newsletter.

---

## Why TikTok and Instagram for this product

The target user — a recruiter using Claude.ai Pro daily — is not on GitHub or HN. They're on TikTok watching "things I do as a recruiter" content and on Instagram following HR thought leaders. This is where the discovery moment happens.

The content style that works: short, specific, first-person, screen-recorded. Not polished. Polished content from a "brand" performs worse than authentic content from a person.

**Golden rule:** Never talk about The Marqet in TikTok/Instagram content. Talk about how you use Claude. The link to /recruiter goes in the bio. Interested people find it.

---

## TikTok content strategy

**Account:** Personal account, not a brand account. Recruiter posting about how they use AI at work.
**Posting frequency:** 3–5 videos per week minimum for the first 4 weeks. TikTok rewards consistency more than quality.
**Format mix:** 60% educational/tutorial, 30% storytelling/personal, 10% trend-based.

### Video formats that work

**1. "Watch me" screen recordings**
Show Claude in real time. No explanation needed — just show the input and the output.
- "Watch me write a job description in 4 minutes with Claude"
- "Watch me generate an interview scorecard live"
- "Watch me fix this terrible JD with Claude" (use a bad JD from a real job board, anonymised)
- "Watch me write 3 rejection emails in under 5 minutes"
- "Watch me prep for a candidate interview in 8 minutes"

**2. "Things I no longer do manually" list format**
Fast-cut, text overlay, trending audio. Works reliably.
- Part 1: Writing job descriptions from scratch
- Part 2: Formatting interview scorecards
- Part 3: Agonising over rejection email wording
- Part 4: Drafting offer letters
- Part 5: Writing performance review templates

**3. Before/After**
Show the old way vs. the Claude way. Side by side or sequential.
- Bad JD → Claude JD
- Generic rejection email → Claude rejection email
- Blank scorecard → structured scorecard with rubric

**4. "Day in my life" recruiting with Claude**
Vlog-style, phone propped up at desk. Show a full morning using Claude for real tasks.
Works because it's aspirational — people see a workflow they want.

**5. Hot take / controversial opinion**
- "Unpopular opinion: recruiters who don't use AI are making their jobs harder than they need to be"
- "The problem with most job descriptions isn't the length — it's this"
- "Stop using ChatGPT for recruiting. Use this instead"
- "AI won't replace recruiters. But recruiters who use AI will replace recruiters who don't."

**6. Tutorial / how-to**
Step-by-step, slightly longer (45–60 seconds). Higher production value OK here.
- "How to set up Claude for recruiting in 3 minutes"
- "The Claude trick that saves me hours every week (system prompts explained)"
- "How I use Claude.ai Projects to work like I have a junior researcher"

**7. Reactional / duet / stitch**
React to HR/recruiting content from other creators or news articles.
- Stitch a recruiter saying "writing JDs is the worst part of my job" → "let me show you how I fixed this"
- React to a bad AI-generated JD with "here's how you avoid this with the right setup"

## Instagram strategy

**Account:** Same person account as TikTok (or a dedicated page once you have 1K+ followers).
**Content mix:** 40% Reels (repurposed from TikTok), 30% Carousels, 20% Stories, 10% Static posts.

### Instagram-specific formats

**Carousel: "X prompts for recruiters"**
Best-performing organic format on Instagram. Each slide = one prompt title/description.
- "6 Claude prompts every recruiter should save" (show each prompt title, teaser of the text)
- "How I set up Claude for recruiting — 5 slides"
- "Before and after: the same job description, with and without Claude"
- "The 5 most common recruiting tasks Claude handles for me"

**Story series**
- "Claude tip of the day" — daily story, 5 days a week
- "Prompt of the week" — share one prompt with context on how to use it
- Polls: "Do you use AI for JDs?" → follow up with the tutorial

**Reels (from TikTok)**
Repurpose every TikTok video as an Instagram Reel. Use the same caption with hashtags.
